Privacy Policy

App: Offline Tasks (com.codemine.offlinetasks)
Last updated: 2 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Offline Tasks app ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Data You Create

Tasks, notes, reminders, and any content you enter are stored only on your device. This data is never transmitted to us or to any server. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage permanently deletes this data.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to any personally identifiable information. We do not require an account, sign-in, or registration.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process certain information to serve and measure ads, including your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and general device information. This data is handled by Google, not by us.

You can learn how Google uses this data here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can reset or limit ad personalization in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads.

4. Notifications

The App may schedule local reminder notifications on your device. These are generated on-device and are not sent to or through any external server.

5. Permissions

The App requests the Internet permission solely to load advertisements.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
